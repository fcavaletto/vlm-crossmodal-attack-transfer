# Prior-art matrix

This matrix maps claims a reader might attribute to Study 2 onto sources opened on 6 November 2026. Bibliographic detail is in [`LITERATURE_REVIEW.md`](LITERATURE_REVIEW.md). "Open relative to these sources" means the opened pages do not already report the estimate. It is not a global novelty search.

Multi-context PGD is the row labeled Multi-P. It is a published baseline. H\* in [`RESEARCH_PROPOSAL.md`](RESEARCH_PROPOSAL.md) does not depend on inventing it.

## How to read the matrix

Rows 1 through 5 are why multi-context PGD is not a contribution. Row 9 is why "alignment predicts transfer" is not a contribution as a general slogan. Row 22 is the contribution that would remain if the experiment is later run and the interval for β_A lies above 0: a particular functional, on a particular leakage-controlled pair, for a particular endpoint.

Rows 13 and 14 are why "gradient conflict" and "which modality wins" need a definition in the paper. The definition used here is \(A(s, t) < 0\): a signed image step that increases the source answer-loss decreases the target answer-loss to first order.
